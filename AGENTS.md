# Repository instructions

## Readability and ownership

At the start of work, identify the intended reader, their task, and the relevant
customer, user, and system flow. Use concrete actions and consistent terms.
Explain necessary technical names before relying on them, while keeping API,
command, and data identifiers exact.

Before finishing, check that another person can understand the result, follow
its instructions, recognize success or failure, locate the responsible code,
and maintain or share it without the chat. Preserve reasons, limits, and
verification evidence. Fix essential explanation gaps as part of the work;
readability is a condition of completion.
